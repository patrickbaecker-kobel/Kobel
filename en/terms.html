5. Shared household

Anyone who becomes a member of your household can – depending on their role – see, change and delete household data: tasks, lists, contacts, finances and much more. Adults see everything except what is marked “Private”, “only me” or visible only to selected people. Children see less.

Joining works with a code: whoever enters it sends a request that an adult member has to confirm. An adult can also invite someone by email address; whoever signs in with that address joins the household without further confirmation (the invitation is valid for 30 days). Enter the address carefully.

Only pass the join code to people you genuinely want to give this access to. You can renew the code at any time (the old one then stops working) and remove members.

You can link your household with another one, send meal invitations and share trips with other households. The other household then sees what is intended for this (see the Privacy Policy, section 3.9). Titles and notes are free text – only write what the other household may read. You can end links and guests at any time.
